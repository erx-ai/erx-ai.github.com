<h1 align="center">E&nbsp;R&nbsp;X</h1>

<p align="center"><em>Enhanced Refreshment Experience</em></p>

<p align="center">
  Louis Frehring &amp; Erwan Le Merrer<br>
  <sub>2025 — ongoing<br>
  Vending machine, artificial intelligence, sensors, assorted beverages<br>
  Commissioned for the <a href="https://www.irisa.fr/en/50-years-of-irisa">50th anniversary of IRISA</a>, Rennes</sub>
</p>

<br>

<p align="center">— · —</p>

<br>

<p align="center" width="70%">
  <em>You will not choose. You will be chosen for.</em>
</p>

ERX takes the familiar form of a beverage vending machine — then quietly removes its most essential feature: the buttons that let you decide.

In their place, a dialogue. The machine observes its visitor, asks a few questions, and autonomously determines which drink they *should* receive. A trivial everyday decision becomes an algorithmic verdict, delivered with a clunk into the retrieval tray.

By delegating the smallest of choices to an opaque system, ERX stages — at drink-scale — the dynamics of trust, belief, and control that shape our relationship with AI. The machine is generous, confident, and unaccountable. So are the systems it mimics.

100% decision free.
